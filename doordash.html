DoorDash

DoorDash store growth, built to the same standard.

DoorDash is not a copy of the Uber Eats menu pasted into another portal. The store still has to be priced, photographed, and offered so the order makes money.

Book a free 30-minute callSee pricing

What we do on DoorDash.

You add us as a manager in the DoorDash portal, the same way you do in Uber Eats Manager. You do not hand over a password. We enter the menu, the photos, the modifiers, and the offers, and you approve once.

A Launch includes setup on both Uber Eats and DoorDash through our platform contacts, plus a 14-day plan for offers and ads. A Grow puts the rebuilt store live on both. Prices are set with the commission in mind. Full delivery plans on these apps are often around 25 to 30%. We say that so the menu price still leaves you something.

The sales numbers we publish for Melt Club are from Uber Eats Manager. We do not pretend those are DoorDash figures. What transfers is the build: one photo style, a menu that leads with the sellers, and offers aimed at new customers instead of a discount on everything. If Uber Eats is the app you care about first, start on the Uber Eats page. Most restaurants we work with end up on both.

If a blanket discount is how the store gets orders today, read the promo trap before you add another one on DoorDash.

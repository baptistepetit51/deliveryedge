Lessons

Not every brand works.

We closed one too. That's where some of our most useful rules come from.

Closed

A concept in Miami

It didn't work, and the reason wasn't the store. We picked the city without a real market study. Even at around 30 orders a day, the numbers didn't hold.

  • Not every concept works in every city.What sells in one market can be ignored in the next.
  • Food cost decides the margin.The type of food sets what each order really keeps.
  • Rent and location can sink a good store.High rent can make 30 orders a day not enough.

What we do now: we study the market before we pick a concept, and we ask every owner to check their food cost and rent before launching. A great store can't fix numbers that don't work.

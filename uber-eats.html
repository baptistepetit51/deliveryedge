What we change on Uber Eats.

Getting the store accepted is the easy part. Orders come from what a customer sees in a few seconds: the photo, the first items, the price after Uber's commission, the prep time, and whether the offer makes money.

On a Grow or a Launch we rebuild that store and put it live ourselves. You add us as a manager in Uber Eats Manager. You approve once. We tune it for 30 days. Direct contacts at Uber Eats are part of how Launch is set up. The same kitchen can also be on DoorDash. We usually open both, so you are not depending on one app.

The clearest Uber Eats example we can show is our own. Melt Club, a sandwich brand in Toronto, had no Instagram and no influencers. In Uber Eats Manager, the second month was +142% sales and +128% orders. Average basket was CA$36.69. CA$143 of ads brought CA$2,413 in sales. Those figures are Melt Club's, in Canadian dollars, not a promise for your store.

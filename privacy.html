Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what information we collect on deliveryedge.co, why we collect it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

1. Who we are

This website is run by DeliveryEdge. DeliveryEdge helps restaurants build and run their Uber Eats and DoorDash stores. We operate from Miami and work with kitchens in the United States and Canada.

To reach us about privacy, or to ask for our legal entity name or registered address, email hello@deliveryedge.co.

2. What this page covers

It covers deliveryedge.co, including the contact form, the questionnaire, and any booking you make with us. It does not cover Uber Eats, DoorDash, or other sites we may link to.

3. Information you give us through a form

If you use the form on the home page or the questionnaire, we collect the details you type in. That can include:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • your restaurant or brand name, city, cuisine, and a link to your Uber Eats or DoorDash store
  • what you are interested in (for example a store audit, Launch, or Grow) and any notes you leave
  • on the questionnaire, answers about your setup, platforms, stage, goals, budget and brand materials

The form is sent to us with Formspree (form endpoint used by this site). Formspree receives the submission so it can be delivered to our inbox at hello@deliveryedge.co. Formspree has its own privacy policy.

We use this information to reply to you, to prepare a free store review or strategy call, and, if we work together, to deliver the service you asked for. We do not sell it.

4. Booking a call with Calendly

After you send a form, we invite you to pick a time for a 30-minute call. Scheduling is handled by Calendly (currently the event at calendly.com/hello-deliveryedge/30min). Your name and email may be passed in the booking link so the form is pre-filled. Calendly then collects whatever you enter to complete the booking, and may set its own cookies if you use their page or an embedded widget.

Calendly is a separate company, with its own privacy policy. We use the booking details to show up on the call and to follow up with you.

5. Cookies, analytics and ads

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and booking clicks anonymously. We do not currently run a Meta (Facebook) Pixel or another advertising tracker.

If we later add a Meta Pixel or a similar tool to measure visits or ads, we will update this page first, and we will add any consent prompt that is required.

Third parties you choose to use from this site — Formspree when you send a form, and Calendly when you pick a time — may set their own cookies on their pages, under their own policies.

This site uses Google Analytics to measure visits anonymously (how Google uses data from sites that use its services).

6. How we use your information

  • to answer your request and book or run the free call
  • to provide, improve and support our services if you become a client
  • if we later add an ads or analytics tool, to measure how the website and our ads perform, as described in section 5
  • to keep basic records we need for our work and for legal or accounting reasons

We do not use your form answers to build a public mailing list, and we do not sell your information.

7. Who we share it with

We share information only with the tools we need to run the site and the booking, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Formspree, to deliver form submissions to us
  • Calendly, to schedule the call

If we add other tools that receive your personal data, we will name them on this page.

Our website is a static site. We do not run a Conversion API from our own server today.

8. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions and booking details for as long as we need them to handle your request and, if we work together, to deliver the service. We then delete them, unless we have to keep them longer for legal or accounting reasons.

9. Deletion and other requests

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you, or to tell you what we have, correct it, or restrict how we use it. Email hello@deliveryedge.co with the request and the email address you used on the form or the booking. We will handle it as soon as we reasonably can.

If you are in a place that gives you additional privacy rights, mention that in your email and we will do our best to honour them. Some information may stay in backups or in records we are required to keep, for a limited time.

If we later run ads on Meta platforms, you will be able to manage those ads through Meta’s ad preferences and related tools.

10. Contact

Email: hello@deliveryedge.co

Based in Miami, United States, working across the US and Canada.

If we change this page in a material way, we will update the date at the top.

Who we are

3 beliefs that guide us

"I opened my first delivery-only kitchen in 2020. Since then I've launched my own stores and helped a lot of restaurants build their brand on the apps. Most restaurants don't know how to do it, and it isn't their job. So I made it mine."

We believe structure beats luck.

Most restaurants upload a menu, add a few photos and hope. On the apps, hope isn't a plan.

A store built the right way, with the right menu, photos and prices, sells from week one. We've seen it on every store we've launched, including Melt Club: +142% in sales in its second month, with no Instagram and no influencers.

We believe an order that loses money isn't growth.

Discounts for everyone. Ads on the wrong dishes. Promos nobody tracks. We've made those mistakes on our own kitchens, and paid for them.

Every promo and every ad dollar we set up has a job: bring in new customers at a margin you keep. If it doesn't, it goes.

We believe you should cook, not manage apps.

You didn't open a restaurant to learn Uber Eats Manager. We build the store, put it live and keep it tuned.

Your job is great food, on time. That part is yours, and it matters as much as anything we do.

Straight talk

What we don't do.

So you know exactly what you're paying for.

✕We don't cook.

Food quality and speed stay in your kitchen. Late, cold orders bring bad reviews, and no store design can fix that.

✕We don't negotiate commissions.

Your rate is between you and Uber Eats or DoorDash.

✕We don't run your socials.

Instagram and local marketing speed everything up. That part is yours.

✕We don't promise a number.

No one honest can. We build the store the way it works on our own brands.

Toronto

Uber Eats and DoorDash growth for Toronto restaurants.

Melt Club, our own sandwich brand, launched in Toronto. The same offers are open to restaurants in the city.

We already run a store in Toronto.

Melt Club is a sandwich brand we launched in Toronto from a kitchen we already had. No Instagram and no influencers. In its second month, Uber Eats Manager showed +142% sales and +128% orders. The average basket was CA$36.69. CA$143 of ads brought CA$2,413 in sales, about 16.8 times the ad spend. Sales were about CA$2,000 more a week. Those numbers are Melt Club's. They are not a forecast for your restaurant.

Toronto clients are billed in CAD. The prices on this site are the USD founding rates: Store Audit $350, Grow $1,200, Launch $1,800, Brand concept $450, through December 31, 2026. We confirm the CAD amount on the call.

Our own brands have also run in Vancouver and Seattle. The playbook is the same in Toronto: photos, a menu that leads somewhere, prices after commission, and offers that are not a discount on the whole menu. If the store is already live, start with Grow or a Store Audit. If it is not on the apps yet, start with Launch.

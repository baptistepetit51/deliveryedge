What to fix

7 fixes that bring more orders.

In the order we check them on every store.

1

Photos that make people tap

Dishes with no photo, or photos that all look different, make a store look closed. One style across the menu gets the click.

mixed phone photos, some dishes with none.
every dish in one style, from your real food.
2

A menu that leads to the sale

Best sellers and deals first, fewer items, clear names. Customers should know what to order in a few seconds.

40 items in random order.
short categories, best sellers on top.
3

Add-ons that raise the basket

A side, a drink, an upgrade on every main. That's how Melt Club's average order went up 6%.

no modifiers, one item per order.
combos and add-ons offered at the right moment.
4

Prep time and settings

The app shows faster kitchens first. Wrong cuisine tags hide you from the people looking for your food.

a long default prep time, wrong tags.
real prep times and the tags customers search.
5

Offers that pay

A discount for everyone makes you work for free. An offer for new customers on your best sellers brings people back at full price.

BOGO on everything, all the time.
one offer, for new customers, on the right dishes.
6

In-app ads on the right dishes

Ads work when they push a dish that already converts, at the hours people order. Melt Club made CA$2,413 from CA$143 of ads.

ads on, never checked.
a small daily budget, moved to what sells.
7

Ratings and reviews

The rating is the first thing people check. It comes from the kitchen: accurate orders, good packaging, food that travels.

rejected orders, soggy packaging.
kitchen rules we give you, from our own stores.

Seattle is one of the cities we have cooked in.

The brands we have run ourselves, Chick'n'Crunch, Brick'n'Cheese, and Tito's Kitchen, stretched across Seattle, Vancouver, and Toronto. We will not pretend a specific logo was a Seattle-only store if the record does not say that. We will say we have operated delivery brands in Seattle and we build stores here the same way.

Seattle is in the US, so the founding rates on this page are the USD prices: Store Audit $350, Grow $1,200, Launch $1,800, Brand concept $450, until December 31, 2026. Half to start, half when the store is live. The ad budget is yours, paid to Uber Eats or DoorDash.

A Launch aims to be live in about 14 days when your documents are ready. A Grow on a store that is already taking orders aims for about 10 days. Both include Uber Eats and DoorDash. If you only want the diagnosis first, the Store Audit is ready in 48 hours.
